## Purpose

This document defines the business requirements for Sprint 01 of the ABC Trading Azure Migration Project.

The objective of this sprint is to design the Azure Enterprise Hierarchy that will serve as the foundation for the entire Azure environment.

## Business Scenario

ABC Trading is an international trading company.

- Headquarters: Portugal
- Branch office: Germany
- Employees: 50
- The company is planning to migrate its infrastructure from an on-premises datacenter to Microsoft Azure using Microsoft
  Cloud Adoption Framework ( CAF) best practices.

  Before deploying any Azure resources, the company needs a well-designed Azure governance structure.


## Business Requirements

 The Azure environment must provide:

  - A scalable enterprise hierarchy.
  - Clear separation between production, non-production, and Shared resources .
  - Centralized governance across the Azure environment.
  - Consistent resource organization.
  - Easy administration.
  - Support for future expansion.

   No Azure resources will be deployed during this Sprint.
  
## Success Criteria

 Sprint 01 will be considered complete when :

- The Azure Enterprise Hierarchy is designed.
- The architecture diagram is completed.
- The hierarchy is ready for implementation in the next sprint.
